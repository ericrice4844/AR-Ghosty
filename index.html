Before you go looking

This game contains jumpscares, sudden loud sounds, flashing lights, vibration and disturbing imagery and voices. Some ghosts only appear in the dark.

  • Play somewhere you know, with nothing to trip over. Don't walk while looking at your screen.
  • Never go anywhere you're not allowed to be, and don't play alone outside at night.
  • If you are sensitive to flashing images, turn jumpscares off in Settings.

Camera, microphone and motion are used only on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Speech recognition may be processed by your browser's provider.

Prepare the lens

Each ghost has its own rules. Some need darkness. Some must be heard. Some can only be heard through headphones.

  1. Motion & compassSo the lens knows where you're pointing.
  2. SoundTurn your volume up. You should hear a chime.
  3. Headphones (optional)Put them on, then answer which ear you hear knocking in.
  4. Location (optional)Haunts near you will come from their real direction.

For the darker ones: turn the lights off, or use only your flashlight.
